SAN DIEGO–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Engrail Therapeutics™ (Engrail) today announced that it has acquired NeuroCycle Therapeutics, a company focused on sub-type selective GABA-A modulation. The acquisition strengthens Engrail’s presence in the GABA-A space and provides a strong platform for initiation of clinical trials with multiple assets in 2021. “We believe Engrail represents the ideal company to carry on the work NeuroCycle started given their focus and experience in the GABA-A space.
