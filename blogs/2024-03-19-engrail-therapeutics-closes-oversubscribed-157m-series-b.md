SAN DIEGO – March 19, 2024 – Engrail Therapeutics™ (Engrail) (the Company), a precision neuroscience company focused on the development of transformational therapies to improve the lives of patients, today announced the close of an oversubscribed $157 million Series B financing round. The round was co-led by new investors F-Prime Capital, Forbion, and Norwest Venture Partners, with participation from RiverVest Venture Partners, Red Tree Venture Capital, funds managed by abrdn Inc., Ysios Capital, Longwood Fund, Eight Roads Ventures, and existing founding investor Pivotal Life Sciences.
