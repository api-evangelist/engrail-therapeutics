SAN DIEGO–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Engrail Therapeutics™ (Engrail) (the Company), a precision neuroscience company focused on the acquisition, development, and commercialization of transformational therapies, today announced initiation of a multi-center phase 2 clinical trial, the ENCALM Trial, to evaluate the efficacy and safety of ENX-102 in patients with generalized anxiety disorder (GAD). ENX-102 is an investigational, highly selective GABA-A α2,3,5 positive allosteric modulator (PAM) and antagonist at α1-containing GABA-A channels.
