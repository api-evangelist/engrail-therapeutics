SAN DIEGO–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Engrail Therapeutics™ (Engrail) (the Company), a neuroscience company focused on the acquisition, development and commercialization of patient-centric therapies, today announced two key executive appointments. Kimberly Vanover, Ph.D. has been named chief scientific officer, leading the building and advancement of the Company’s pipeline.
