SAN DIEGO, Sept. 18, 2024 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Engrail Therapeutics, a precision neuroscience company focused on the development of transformational therapies to improve the lives of patients, today announced initiation of a Phase 1 single ascending dose clinical study for ENX-104 in healthy volunteers. ENX-104 is a potent and selective dopamine D2/D3 receptor antagonist designed to enhance dopamine neurotransmission by preferential autoreceptor inhibition at low doses.
