SAN DIEGO, August 8, 2024 -- Engrail Therapeutics, a precision neuroscience company focused on the development of transformational therapies to improve the lives of patients, today announced the appointment of Paul Cayer as chief financial officer, and Saira Ramasastry to the company’s board of directors. Ms. Ramasastry will assume the position of chair of the audit committee.
